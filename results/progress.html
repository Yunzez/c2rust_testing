The core idea

STU = the differential-testing frontier. C2Rust restructures code, so fuzzing at an arbitrary function makes "divergences" that are really bad-boundary artifacts. The STU selector finds boundaries where a differential harness is valid (inputs constructible, outputs comparable, no UB) — and the classifier proves every divergence with independent evidence.
Novelty (literature-checked): not the fuzzing, but the static-analysis boundary selection + an evidence-backed divergence taxonomy.
