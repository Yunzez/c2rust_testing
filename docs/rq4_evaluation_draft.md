# RQ4 — proposed rename and data (draft for `evaluation.tex`, not applied)

The paper's RQ4 text (as of 2026-09-07) already carries the six-cell paired numbers and the tulip
explanation. Two things are off: the **name** promises a result the data does not give ("coverage
beyond shipped tests" — the validator's total coverage exceeds the suite in one artifact of six), and
the **table** shows six of nineteen cells, so the funnel, the thirteen unpaired cells, and what the
campaigns' candidates became are invisible in the RQ.

## Name

Current: *RQ4: Coverage Beyond Shipped Tests* — "How much code does our differential validator
exercise beyond the test suites shipped with existing translators and translated libraries?"

Proposed: **RQ4: Reach of the Generated Campaign** (alternatives: *Exploration Relative to
Acceptance Suites*; *What the Validator Reaches, and What Bounds It*).

Question: *Which translated code does a fixed-budget campaign of generated harnesses reach — relative to
the suite that accepted the translation where one exists — and what bounds it?*

Why this name: it is a statement about **reach and its bounds**, which is what every cell measures
(19 cells report reach; 6 report it against a baseline), and it lets the honest headline stand:
functions equal, regions lower, unique code on both sides, one named cause per gap (construction
failures for cJSON × PtrTrans, the option domain for tulip). "Beyond" made the suite the thing to
beat; "reach" makes the suite the ruler.

## Data to show

2. **All nineteen cells** — new `\ValidatorReachTable`, generated by `scripts/rq4/paper_tables.py`
   from `results/rq3_coverage/*/cells.json` + the manifest (`results/rq3_coverage/rq4_tables.tex`,
   paste into `figures_tables.tex`). Columns: suite status · planned/matched · built · corpus ·
   fn/region coverage of the translation's own universe · confirmed div./term. · defects (bold =
   first found here; † = held-out candidate). This is where the funnel (1 223 of 1 922 matched
   boundaries planned, 365 built) and the five new / six re-found defects become visible in the RQ

   `cJSON_New_Item` stub, held as CAND-5) and tulip (functions 212/213 both, regions 0.34 vs 0.93 —
   the option domain; every corpus saturates within a minute).
