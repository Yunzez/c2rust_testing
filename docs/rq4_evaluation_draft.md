# RQ4 — design note (applied to `evaluation.tex` on 2026-09-07)

> RQ4 now uses one table covering all 19 artifacts. The earlier table restricted to the six
> passing-suite cells was removed: availability of a passing suite is a measurement condition,
> not a reason to omit the other thirteen cells.

The paper's RQ4 text (as of 2026-09-07) already carries the six-cell paired numbers and the tulip
explanation. Two things are off: the **name** promises a result the data does not give ("coverage
beyond shipped tests" — the validator's total coverage exceeds the suite in one artifact of six), and
the **table** shows six of nineteen cells, so the funnel, the thirteen unpaired cells, and what the
campaigns' candidates became are invisible in the RQ.

## Name

Former: *RQ4: Coverage Beyond Shipped Tests* — "How much code does our differential validator
exercise beyond the test suites shipped with existing translators and translated libraries?"

Proposed: **RQ4: Reach of the Generated Campaign** (alternatives: *Exploration Relative to
Acceptance Suites*; *What the Validator Reaches, and What Bounds It*).

Question: *Which translated code does a fixed-budget campaign of generated harnesses reach — relative to
the suite that accepted the translation where one exists — and what bounds it?*

Why this name: it is a statement about **reach and its bounds**, which is what every cell measures
(19 cells report reach; 6 report it against a baseline), and it lets the honest headline stand:
functions equal, regions lower, unique code on both sides, one named cause per gap (construction
failures for cJSON × PtrTrans, the option domain for tulip). "Beyond" made the suite the thing to
beat; "reach" makes the suite the ruler.

## Data to show

1. **One table, all nineteen cells** — `\ValidatorReachTable`, generated by
   `scripts/rq4/paper_tables.py` from `results/rq3_coverage/*/cells.json`
   (`results/rq3_coverage/rq4_tables.tex`). It reports suite status; the
   matched/planned/built funnel;
   tests/ours function and region coverage; tests-only/ours-only regions where pairing is valid.
   Defects, held-out candidates, and raw candidate-input counts are excluded because they belong
   to RQ2 rather than reach.
2. **Two sentences of bounds**, each tied to a row: cJSON × PtrTrans (only 9 of 113 matched
   boundaries build because reshaped object interfaces lack a supported bridge) and tulip
   (functions 212/213 both, regions 0.34 vs 0.93 —
   the option domain; every corpus saturates within a minute).

## Suggested paragraph order (replacing the current §RQ4 body; numbers from SUMMARY_ALL.md)

1. Setup (as now): one coverage mechanism, one universe, suite to completion vs 3 600 s campaign,
   four-set partition, a failed suite is never 0 %.
2. `\ValidatorReachTable`, followed first by the paired reading (34.4–81.4 % vs 79.7–93.5 %;
   unique on both sides; tulip's cause).
3. The all-cell reading: what the planner constructs (planned/matched per
   cell, and why the rest fails — `T*` to pointer-carrying structs without a usable producer, reshaped
   signatures, environment inputs), what a fixed campaign reaches against the translation's own
   universe (6.3 % for a translation whose constructor crashes, lil × C2SaferRust, to 87 % for a
   faithful lil). Candidate confirmation and defect counts are reported under RQ2, not here.
4. Answer: the validator reaches what the suites reach at function level and complements them at
   region level; its bounds are construction (what the plan can build) and the input model (the
   domain a derived local hides), both named per cell, neither hidden by an aggregate.
